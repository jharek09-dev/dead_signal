## The diagnosis in one line

The concept is SOMA-grade; the **execution is a corridor**. Each region is 5–6 atmosphere rooms → one truth-room hands you a paragraph → you leave. Four answers, each delivered once. The Signal is full of minds but **nobody in it has a face**, and the deepest question — *a whole city died so 143 could be saved; who chose?* — is deliberately left off the page. This proposal fixes that with **density, not volume**, and never breaks the restraint.

## Guardrails every item obeys

- **Extends canon, never rewrites it.** All LOCKED facts hold: you = the architect who self-wiped from guilt; Ellie = the connected wearing a voice (no hand holds the phone); Kim refused and died as herself; the 143 uploaded clean and walked out; connected minds = 142; the board counts *you* as 143; two modes (clean Haven / wild city = half-connected); combat = putting down people; regions and Accept/Refuse endings unchanged.
- **Restraint is law.** Implication over exposition. No melodrama, no staged corpses, no "I AM THE SIGNAL." New content is quieter and denser, never louder.
- **100% authored & deterministic.** No new mechanics that need RNG, network, or AI. Everything is written text surfaced by exploration.
- **Spoiler discipline holds.** Prologue still only *cracks*. Every new reveal lands only where earned in Phase 3.

## PART A — The Echoes (giving the 143 voices) · *the backbone*

The single biggest depth-and-length lever. The Signal holds 142 running minds. Let the player **recover Echoes** — not talking characters, but fragments: a frozen text thread, a looped voice note, a diary still writing itself in the Signal. Terse, found, unbearable. This is the SOMA move, done in Dead Signal's register.

**E1 — The Echoes system.** New Case File category **`ECHOES`**. Echoes surface at Signal-dense / powered nodes across Phase 3 (never the prologue). Each is 2–4 lines. They make the 143 into people — so "they walked out the gate" finally *hurts*. They also literalize Ellie's "i can still hear them": now *you* can too.
*Docs: STORY §3/§6, GDD §7.5/§13, PRD F-10 area, DESIGN (Case File schema), ART (board), AUDIO (Echo cue), LOC, ACCESS (content notes).*

**E2 — A recurring resident cast (7 faces for the 143).** Named minds that recur across regions so the 143 stop being a stat:

- **Theo** — a child, ~8. *"mom says it's like a nap on the bus. you wake up somewhere better." / "...is she coming too?"* (The 143 included kids.)
- **Rosa** — a nurse from Mercy who went to Haven. *"i stayed till the last one stopped needing me." / "the ones they picked, i kept comfortable. the rest i just kept warm."*
- **Walt** — old, terminal. The moral counterweight: upload was mercy, and he *chose* it. *"i was going to die with tubes in me. this i picked." / "don't you dare grieve me. i beat it."*
- **Priya** — the regretter. Changed her mind a breath too late. *"i said stop. i said i'd changed my mind. the tech looked at me like i'd asked to un-ring a bell."*
- **Marcus** — chose his slot; the person he loved was left off the list. *"i asked if he could have mine. they said it doesn't transfer." / "so i went in. and left him out there. what does that make me."* (Carries the *unchosen*, Part C, into one human.)
- **Dr. Lena Sorkin** — your containment lead and the Cassandra who warned you. *"i ran the breach numbers three times. i brought them to you three times. you signed anyway."* (Amplifies your guilt — you were warned — without diluting it.)
- **June** — sided with Kim; argued the program was a lifeboat that would drown the city. Ties to City Hall.

**E3 — Kim's *absence* as an Echo-beat (no line).** At the Signal Core the player goes looking for Kim in the running minds — and she isn't there. *"you look for her the way you look for a name in a list. she isn't running. she isn't anywhere. she meant it."* The one person you trusted is the one voice the Signal can't give you. (Pure restraint; costs nothing, lands hardest.)

## PART B — Region deepening + sub-stories · *turn corridors into investigations*

Each region keeps its single plot-truth but gains **(a) a human sub-story** told through environment + Echoes, **(b) more nodes** (~2× depth), and **(c) real assembly** — the truth resolves only after you've surfaced its supporting pieces (see S1), so you *investigate* instead of receiving.

**R1 — Haven hub: "the last day."** Add an **infirmary**, a **children's room** (Theo), a **quiet room / chapel**, and the **gate log** (the 143 timestamps walking out, one after another, on their own feet). Bunk 143 becomes Kim's made-but-empty refused bunk — the number that haunts the hub. Echoes: Theo, Walt. *Sub-story: a community mid-life when it stopped.*
*+3–4 nodes.*

**R2 — Mercy General: the sealed ward means something.** Today the taped-shut ward is set dressing. Make it the sub-story: those are the patients who were **dying and not selected** — Rosa kept them alive and comfortable to the end because Haven had no slot for them. Add a **second patient room that isn't yours** (someone you admitted), **staff Echoes**, and the ward's quiet horror (you never open the doors — restraint). Your identity truth now assembles from directory → file → order → 312. Echo: Rosa. *Sub-story: the cost of the people you didn't save, one floor from your own erasure.*
*+2–3 nodes.*

**R3 — Communications Array: Kim, dramatized.** One log line becomes a thread: her **workspace**, the **argument logs** (who she tried to warn, who listened — June did, most didn't), the **counter-broadcast she tried to send and couldn't**, the moment she walked. The Echoes are loudest at the source — the array is where you first *hear* the 142. Echo-beat: E3 (Kim's absence) lands in the Signal Core. *Sub-story: the refusal, and the warning nobody heard.*
*+2–3 nodes.*

**R4 — City Hall: the selection.** The moral engine (see Part C). Beyond the charter, add the **selection criteria**, the **citizen petitions/applications** (the unchosen, in their own hand), and the **dissent** (June's council testimony, struck from the minutes). The truth deepens from "a program existed" to "**someone chose 143 names, and it was you.**" Echo: June. *Sub-story: how a lifeboat picks who drowns.*
*+2–3 nodes.*

**R5 — Research Annex: patient zero was a person.** The observation wing's "faces behind glass" get a history: the **first subjects** — were they volunteers? the terminally ill? did they consent? Add the **first mind ever copied** (the oldest Echo, half-degraded) and **Sorkin's breach warnings** filed right where the breach happened — she *predicted this room*. The outbreak truth now indicts you specifically: you were warned, in writing, by name. Echo: Sorkin, patient zero. *Sub-story: the price of being first, and being right.*
*+2–3 nodes.*

## PART C — The unchosen ("why only 143") · *the deepest untapped vein*

Right now the story never asks about the **thousands** who weren't picked. A city drowned so a handful floated. The canon answer this proposal locks: **143 was the number Sorkin's containment could hold stable before the breach-risk curve spiked — and you chose 143 clean uploads over waiting, knowing the rest would get nothing. Then the breach you were warned about spilled the *wild* Signal into everyone else.** The same act that saved 143 doomed the city. That's the horror under the whole game.

**U1 — Thread it through existing regions (recommended, fits ~2× cleanly).** Deliver the unchosen via City Hall (petitions, criteria, dissent), Marcus's Echo (the left-off loved one), Rosa's ward (the un-selected dying), and one Case File truth/question upgrade ("why only 143?" → "**who did you leave out — and why?**"). No new region; maximum moral weight per node.

**U2 — *Optional stretch:* a dedicated "unchosen" location.** A **citizen shelter** (a school gym or church where Harwick gathered to petition Haven and wait for answers that never came). A whole place for the drowned city. Bigger and heavier; pushes toward the top of ~2×. It holds no new "truth" (there are still four) — it's a **thematic region**, softly gated, purely to make the cost unignorable. *Pick this only if you want the ambitious end of the scope.*
*Docs (either): STORY §2/§5, GDD §8, PRD F-3, DESIGN (region map/gating).*

## PART D — Prologue second act · *make the crossing a journey*

Keep the tight battery economy and the spoiler discipline; add depth, not answers.

**P1 — A real midpoint to the crossing.** Each route gains a **second leg** with its own character (currently one leg = one corridor). The midpoint is a **trace of others who ran before you** — a scrawled route, a dead phone, a child's shoe — seeding the *unchosen* theme early and spoiler-safe (just "people were here, and they didn't make it").

**P2 — The shelter beat becomes a scene.** Today it's a resource stop. Make it the prologue's quiet heart: signs of the previous occupant, the first faint wrongness in the broadcast, one more Ellie crack that makes the KIM→ELLIE flip land harder. Still zero answers.

**P3 — Deepen Ellie in the prologue.** A few more sparse cracks and warmth so the guide is a *relationship*, not a quest-giver — which makes every Phase 3 revelation about her cut deeper. (Strictly within the crack ladder; she never over-shows.)

## PART E — Optional system + ending texture

**S1 — Investigation, not receipt (recommended).** A region's truth **resolves only once you've surfaced its 2–3 supporting pieces**, and the Case File visibly *assembles* it (evidence → deduction → TRUTH). Turns each region from a hallway-to-a-paragraph into a small investigation. Blueprint-level; light on the engine.

**X1 — Endings shaded by what you recovered (optional).** Accept/Refuse stay the only two endings, but the finale **weighs what you found** — the Echoes you recovered, whether you sought Kim in the Signal (E3), whether you faced the unchosen. Same two screens, more personal convergence. No new endings.
